# Credits

## Orbits
Orbital elements (GP/TLE) and the satellite catalogue (SATCAT) come from **[CelesTrak](https://celestrak.org)**, maintained by Dr T.S. Kelso, using data from the US Space Force's public catalogue. Please follow [CelesTrak's usage guidance](https://celestrak.org/NORAD/documentation/gp-data-formats.php); the workflow downloads the catalogue once a day.

## Stars and Milky Way
Star positions, magnitudes and colours, and the Milky Way outline, come from **[d3-celestial](https://github.com/ofrohn/d3-celestial)** by Olaf Frohn (BSD-3-Clause), derived from the Hipparcos catalogue.

## Land
Shaded relief, borders, populated places and peaks come from **[Natural Earth](https://www.naturalearthdata.com)**, which is public domain.
